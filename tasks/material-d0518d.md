If the ring beam plus view tilt still reads as too flat or too linear, try other light through the glass on focus: a plane (sheet) of light sweeping through the slab, shafts or beams crossing it, or a rotated environment whose reflection moves across the glint. The user has no preference between moving the camera, the objects, or both; judge by effect.
