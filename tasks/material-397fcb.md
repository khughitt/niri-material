Lower the cost of adding a material: review the config surface, shader registry, uniforms, solver hooks, and docs a new material touches today and collapse the boilerplate. Goal is a path a community contributor could follow to add a material without reading the whole pipeline, with an eye toward future user contributions. Candidate first users: ice and rainbow/aurora glass.
